# Brag Plan: Veritas Credit Underwriting Portal

## What is this app?
An institutional-grade, AI-powered credit underwriting portal that assesses loan eligibility in under 200ms using calibrated Gradient Boosting, Explainable AI (XAI), and forensic bank statement cash-flow analysis.

## The angle
Underwriting without black-box guesswork. Veritas brings auditable, fair-lending compliant machine learning to loan decisions with real-time explainability (+/- percentage points) and automated credit memorandums.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"AI Underwriting Without the Black Box."
Bold serif editorial text rising onto warm alabaster with deep emerald accents, establishing institutional banking authority.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Instant Risk Scoring & Decision**: Live calibrated approval probability (e.g. 85.3% ROC-AUC) and clean decision badge (Approved / Low Risk).
- **Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution**: Animated diverging horizontal bars showing exact factor contributions (+12.4pp Credit Score, -4.2pp DTI).
- **Forensic Cash-Flow & NTC Parser**: Automated 3-layer bank statement ingestion extracting ABB, salary credits, and detecting ECS/NACH bounces for thin-file borrowers.

## Outro / punchline
"Veritas. Transparent Credit Decisions."
Clean logo lockup, live GitHub link, and audit seal.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Institutional editorial banking aesthetic (warm alabaster, deep forest green, serif typography)
- Interpretation: Confident, crisp, authoritative, with smooth mathematical precision.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds
